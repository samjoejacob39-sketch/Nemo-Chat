Terms & policies
Preview draft · v0.1
01 · Terms of service

Using Nemo

By creating an account and using Nemo, you agree to follow these terms. Nemo is a messaging app designed to help people communicate with friends and groups.

You must provide information that is reasonably accurate, keep your sign-in details private, and use the app in a lawful and considerate way. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account.

02 · Your account

Keep your account secure

Choose a username that does not impersonate another person. Keep your password and recovery codes private. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or account may be able to see your conversations.

Recovery codes are intended to help you regain access. Store them somewhere private and secure. A code may stop working after it has been used or replaced.

03 · Community guidelines

Be respectful

Do not use Nemo to threaten, harass, bully, exploit, or impersonate others. Do not share content that is unlawful, abusive, or intended to harm someone. Respect other people's boundaries and privacy.

Do not attempt to disrupt the service, access another person's account, or misuse reporting and request features. We may review reports and take appropriate action under the final published policies.

04 · Messages and groups

Your conversations

You are responsible for the text, images, and other material you choose to share. Only share content you have the right to share, and think carefully before sending private or sensitive information.

Group participants may copy, save, or share messages outside a group. Do not assume every recipient will keep a message confidential.

05 · Privacy summary

How information is used

Nemo needs some account and service information to operate features such as sign-in, friend requests, notifications, and messaging. The final Privacy Policy must explain exactly what is collected, why it is used, how long it is kept, and what choices you have.

Do not assume messages are end-to-end encrypted. Encryption, access controls, retention periods, and data deletion practices must be described accurately in the published Privacy Policy once implemented and verified.

06 · Availability and changes

Service updates

Features may change as Nemo is improved. The service may occasionally be unavailable for maintenance, updates, or technical reasons. We will publish the final process for account support, suspension, and deletion before public launch.

07 · Contact and final policy

Questions or concerns?

For the public release, Nemo must provide a working support contact and complete legal policy details. Those details have not yet been supplied in this prototype.
